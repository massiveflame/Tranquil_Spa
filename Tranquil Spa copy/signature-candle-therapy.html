Signature Candle Therapy

Warm Soy Wax & Aromatherapy | 75 Minutes

Exclusive Ritual

Sensory Warmth & Hydration

Welcome to Tranquil International Spa, your sanctuary of relaxation in Bengaluru. Step into a world of sensory delight with our exclusive Signature Candle Therapy.

Signature Candle Therapy is a unique, deeply calming treatment that combines the soothing elements of massage, heat therapy, and aromatherapy. Special therapeutic massage candles are lit prior to your treatment, filling the room with premium botanical fragrances and melting into a warm, mineral-rich oil.

This cosmetic oil is formulated with a luxury blend of pure soy wax, organic shea butter, and essential oils. The melted warm liquid is then slowly poured onto your body and massaged into the skin, distributing soothing warmth deep into muscle tissues to melt away stress.

The Warm Pour Technique

Your therapist will light the signature candles, letting their soothing glow fill the suite. As the candle wax melts into a warm oil, it is gently drizzled over physical tension points in controlled, soothing streams. The therapist utilizes long, gliding strokes to massage this warm, nutrient-rich oil into the muscles. Unlike petroleum-based wax, cosmetic soy wax absorbs completely, leaving your skin soft and hydrated.

Therapeutic Benefits

  • Deep Muscle Warming: Thermal properties of the oil immediately relax stiff muscle fibers.
  • Intense Skin Hydration: Rich shea butter and soy lipids nourish and smooth dry skin.
  • Nervous System Calming: Rhythmic gliding strokes combined with warmth soothe anxiety.
  • Sensory Aromatherapy: Melting essential oils (e.g. lavender, vanilla) soothe mental fatigue.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will the warm candle wax burn my skin? +

No! Massage candles are specifically formulated to melt at a low temperature (about 38-40°C), which is just slightly above body temperature. The melted oil feels pleasantly warm and is completely safe and soothing for the skin.

What is the duration of a Signature Candle session? +

A typical Signature Candle Therapy session at Tranquil International Spa lasts for 75 minutes, allowing ample time for complete full-body application and deep muscle warming massage.

Are the candles chemically safe? +

Absolutely. The candles are cosmetic-grade and handcrafted using 100% natural soy wax, organic shea butter, and pure therapeutic essential oils. They contain zero paraffin, petroleum, or synthetic colors, making them excellent and clean for skin nourishment.

Should I wash the candle oil off after the treatment? +

We recommend leaving the oil on your skin! Because it is made of natural shea butter and soy wax, it functions as a highly nourishing skin mask. It will absorb completely within an hour, leaving your skin soft and glowing without any greasy residue.
