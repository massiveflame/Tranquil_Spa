Balinese Therapy

Rhythmic Acupressure & Energy Flow | 60 - 90 Minutes

Ritual Overview

The Art of Balinese Healing

Welcome to Tranquil International Spa, an exquisite haven of wellness in Bengaluru. Step into the world of traditional Balinese therapy, a time-honored technique designed to restore harmony to the body and mind.

Originating from the beautiful island of Bali, Indonesia, Balinese therapy is deeply rooted in ancient holistic healing traditions. This therapeutic technique is designed to balance the body's energy flow, release deep-seated physical tension, and promote overall spiritual well-being.

Our skilled therapists blend gentle and firm strokes, rhythmic kneading, and palm pressure to stimulate blood circulation and soothe tired muscles. Combined with the use of fragrant essential oils, Balinese therapy creates a sensory journey that rejuvenates both the physical body and the soul.

The Balinese Technique

During the session, your therapist will begin with gentle stretching movements to release joint tension and improve physical flexibility. The therapist then utilizes hands, fingers, and thumbs to apply firm acupressure on specific meridian points to alleviate muscle knots and break down energy blockages, harmonizing your body's natural flow.

Therapeutic Benefits

  • Relieves Muscle Tension: Targeted acupressure and kneading release stiffness and physical soreness.
  • Promotes Relaxation: Rhythmic strokes and soothing aromas induce a deep relaxation state, reducing stress.
  • Improves Blood Circulation: Enhances systemic blood flow, supporting cell oxygenation and skin glowing.
  • Balances Energy: Stimulates acupressure meridians to leave you feeling completely revitalized.
  • Boosts Immunity: Encourages lymphatic circulation to boost overall health defenses.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Balinese therapy suitable for everyone? +

Balinese therapy is generally safe for most individuals. However, if you have any specific health concerns, cardiovascular histories, or medical conditions, please inform your therapist before the session to ensure your safety.

How long does a Balinese therapy session last? +

A typical Balinese therapy session at Tranquil International Spa lasts for 60 to 90 minutes, depending on your chosen booking preference.

What type of oils are used during the therapy? +

We use premium quality, warm organic essential oils (e.g. coconut oil base infused with local frangipani or jasmine extracts), carefully selected to enhance the therapeutic relaxation benefits of Balinese massage.

Can I request a male or female therapist? +

Certainly, we understand personal comfort preferences. You can request a male or female therapist when submitting your booking reservation request, and our coordinators will gladly arrange it.
