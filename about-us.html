About Our Sanctuary

Relax Your Mind, Body & Soul

The Sanctuary

Sowing Silence & Energy

Established in the heart of Bengaluru's vibrant Koramangala district, Tranquil International Spa was created as an escape from structural stress.

Our mission is to sow silence, energy, beauty, and vitality in the lives of our guests. By blending ancient healing methodologies from Bali and Thailand with classical European bodywork systems, we provide a holistic, premium environment that nurtures physical health and mental recovery.

We believe that luxury lies in the details—from the warmth of our cosmetic soy wax pour treatments to the curated organic essential oils diffused in our suites. Step into our sanctuary and let the outside world fade away.

Our Core Values

Quality Standards

Certified Practitioners & Botanical Care

All therapies at Tranquil International Spa are administered by fully certified physical therapists and massage practitioners trained extensively in holistic traditions.

We utilize 100% pure botanical oils, organic cosmetic soy waxes, and allergen-free cosmetic shea butter. We enforce strict sterilization protocols across all treatment suites, hot stone accessories, and steam systems to guarantee a safe, soothing wellness experience.
