Aroma Therapy

Restoring Mind, Body & Soul | 60 Minutes

Ritual Overview

Discover the Power of Aroma

At Tranquil International Spa, we believe in the power of aromatherapy to heal the nervous system and restore physical balance.

Aromatherapy is an ancient holistic practice that utilizes the natural essences of aromatic plants and pure essential oils to promote overall wellness. When inhaled or applied to the skin, these natural plant essences interact with the body's chemistry, triggering emotional and physiological pathways that support recovery.

Our experienced therapists curate a personalized aroma journey for you. By selecting from calming floral notes like lavender to invigorating leaves like eucalyptus, we tailor the oils to alleviate your specific tension points and mental fatigue.

The Method

During your 60-minute session, you will be comfortably reclined in a quiet, heated treatment suite. The therapist gently applies the custom oil blends in long, rhythmic strokes, allowing the skin to absorb active botanical elements while diffusing the scents to fill the room with tranquility.

Therapeutic Benefits

  • Alleviates daily stress, chronic tension, and mild anxiety
  • Promotes deeper sleep and balances emotional states
  • Relieves physical discomforts such as minor headaches and muscle tightness
  • Intensely nourishes and hydrates skin surfaces

Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the duration of an aroma therapy session? +

A typical aroma therapy session at Tranquil International Spa lasts for 60 minutes, allowing you ample time to indulge in the therapeutic benefits of our essential oils.

Are the essential oils safe for all skin types? +

Yes, we use high-quality, pure essential oils that are generally safe for all skin types. However, if you have any allergies or specific skin conditions, please inform your therapist before the session so that we can select the most suitable oils for you.

Can aroma therapy help with stress and anxiety? +

Absolutely! Aroma therapy is renowned for its stress-relieving properties. The soothing scents of essential oils can help alleviate feelings of anxiety, promote relaxation, and improve overall mental well-being.

What should I expect during an aroma therapy session? +

During your aroma therapy session, you will be comfortably reclined as our therapist gently applies the essential oils to your body or uses a diffuser to disperse the scents in the air. Soft music and ambient lighting create a tranquil atmosphere to enhance your experience.

Are aroma therapy sessions suitable for pregnant women? +

For the safety and comfort of pregnant women, we offer specialized pregnancy-safe aroma therapy sessions. These sessions use essential oils that are considered safe during pregnancy.
